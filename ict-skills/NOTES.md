## Page 4: ICT Bible, Ali Khan

- The algorithm is responsible for moving price in the market. It is often referred to as IPDA
  (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm).
- IPDA manipulates price in order to engineer liquidity and offer fair value.
  - ↳ Liquidity above/below old highs and old lows.
  - ↳ Areas of inefficient price action.
- IPDA provides unmatched levels of precision and …
- **AMD** → everything starts in consolidation. IPDA holds price in range to build liquidity above
  the consolidation as buy stops and below it as sell stops. This is called the **accumulation phase**.
- IPDA then reprices above the consolidation range, triggering buy stops. This is known as the
  **Judas Swing**.
- Smart money will pair liquidity from buy stops and offload sell stops. This is known as the
  **distribution phase**.
- This is the process of IPDA **market efficiency paradigm** logic: smart money will sell to
  buyers and buy back from the sellers.
- **FVG** → IPDA not only engineers liquidity by moving price but also by leaving Fair Value Gaps.
  In order to maintain fair value parameters it will reprice to rebalance the inefficiencies to
  offer fair value to both sides.
- **BISI** (Buyside Imbalance, Sellside Inefficiency): only buy side offered.
- **SIBI** (Sellside Imbalance, Buyside Inefficiency): only sell side offered.
- *(margin)* Internal range liquidity

## What the skills add that is NOT in the notes

- **The Indian session times are an adaptation, not ICT's.** ICT's killzones are New York times. For
  NSE/BSE, the skills use: the 09:15–09:30 opening range as the "Judas" window (no trades, from
  "avoid the opening ranges"), an AM killzone of 09:30–11:00, lunch 11:30–13:00 off, and a PM
  killzone of 13:15–14:45 (when London opens, 12:30–13:30 IST), flat by 15:15. These are
  starting points to backtest, not rules from the mentorship.
- **The auto-bias rule** (`bias` command) is the common ICT previous-day-high/low reading: a
  close beyond yesterday's range points that way, a sweep that closes back inside points the other way, and
  an inside day gives no bias. The notes say to write the bias down yourself, so the skill shows the
  facts, suggests a bias, and makes you commit to it.
- **Displacement** is measured as a candle body at least 1.5× the average body of the last 20 candles,
  with the body at least half the candle's range ("quick, not a wick").
