PULSE TRADE: QUICK GUIDE

A trading desk for Indian markets that sits beside the PulsEdge news terminal. In demo mode every price is simulated and every order is a paper trade, so you can learn the desk with no risk. Connecting your Dhan account (coming next) switches it to live prices and real orders.

  • Equity desk: watchlist, chart, order ticket, positions and news. Options desk: Nifty option chain, strategy builder and the volatility edge.
  • Multiple monitors: press ↗ on any panel to open it in its own window and drag it to another screen. All windows stay in sync: pick a stock in one and the others follow.
  • Order ticket: Market, Limit, SL (stop-limit) and SL-M (stop-market). Add a stop-loss and target to make a bracket: when one fills the other is cancelled. "Size by risk" works out the quantity so the stop costs your chosen % of capital, including charges.
  • Volatility edge: India VIX has been above the volatility Nifty went on to deliver on 81% of days since 2008. The panel shows whether today's conditions match the ones where selling options worked best in the backtest.
  • Charges: every fill pays STT, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty, SEBI fee and brokerage, exactly as the NSE schedule.
