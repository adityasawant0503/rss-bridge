Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version. We collect your email when you join the waitlist, start a trial, pay or send feedback. Your watchlist and holdings stay in your own browser and are never sent to us. We don't sell data, run ads or use tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Email address, when you join the waitlist, start a free trial, apply for a founding seat or send feedback, plus the form you used and the time.
  • Messages you send through the feedback or contact forms.
  • Payment details, when you buy a plan: Razorpay shares your name, email, phone number, the plan and the payment ID with us. Card, bank and UPI details stay with Razorpay.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist and holdings, imported broker CSVs, theme, pre-market checklist ticks, trial status and access link are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Why we use it

  • To run your trial or membership and send your access link.
  • To reply to you and send service messages, such as a trial ending or a renewal.
  • To improve the product from feedback.
  • To keep payment and tax records as the law requires.

We only email you about PulsEdge. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Services we use

  • Formspree: receives form submissions (waitlist, trial, feedback, contact).
  • Razorpay: processes payments.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log your IP address for security.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare): deliver fonts and scripts, and see your IP address when they do.
  • Telegram: runs the PulsEdge channel under its own privacy policy.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Waitlist and trial emails are kept until you ask us to delete them, or for up to 24 months after your last contact. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently up to 8 years).

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, withdraw your consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Send requests through the contact page. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Grievances

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, contact us first through the contact page and we'll try to resolve it. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

PulsEdge is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security and changes

The site is served over HTTPS and we limit who can see personal data. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and we'll announce significant changes on the site.
