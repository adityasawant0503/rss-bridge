Cancellation, refunds and delivery

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version. Try it free first. If a paid plan isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund. You can cancel any time, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Free trial first

Every new user can try the full terminal free for 5 NSE trading sessions before paying anything. No payment details are needed.

Cancelling

  • Cancel a monthly or yearly plan any time through the contact page or the link in your Razorpay email.
  • You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

  • First payment: ask within 7 days of your first payment on any plan and we'll refund it in full, no questions asked.
  • Later payments: renewals aren't refunded, but you can cancel so you aren't charged again.
  • Duplicate or failed payments: refunded in full as soon as we spot them or you tell us.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Delivery

PulsEdge is a digital service; nothing is shipped. After payment, your access link to the PULSE terminal is emailed to the address you paid with, usually within a few hours and always within 24 hours. If it hasn't arrived, check spam, then contact us with your Razorpay payment ID.
