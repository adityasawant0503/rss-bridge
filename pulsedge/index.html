    Three verdicts

    One word tells you what to do.

    No sentiment scores, no star ratings. Each verdict carries the reason and a time horizon, so you can disagree with it quickly.

    ACT

    Material to your position

    Results, guidance changes, regulatory orders or deals that change the case for a stock you hold or track.

    e.g. a pharma holding receives a USFDA warning letter for its main plant

    WATCH

    Could become material

    Early signals that need a second data point: a pledge change, a management exit, a draft circular.

    e.g. promoter pledge rises by 4 percentage points in a quarter

    IGNORE

    Noise for your portfolio

    Routine compliance filings, recycled stories and broad market chatter with no new information.

    e.g. Regulation 74(5) certificate, trading window closure notice

    How it works

    From raw feed to a decision.

    1. Pulse reads the feedExchange filings, regulators and wires arrive in one stream, deduplicated across sources.
    2. It classifies the eventCategory, affected listed companies, and a time horizon from intraday to multi-quarter.
    3. It checks your watchlistVerdicts are ranked against what you hold, so a sector-wide story reaches you once.
    4. You decide in 30 secondsRead the one-line reason, open the source filing if you want proof, and move on.
