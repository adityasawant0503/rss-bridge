How it works

A few headlines matter to you. PulsEdge finds them.

  1. 01

    It reads everything, as it lands

    NSE announcements, SEBI and RBI circulars and four newsrooms, checked through the trading day and de-duplicated across sources.

  2. 02

    Each headline gets a verdict

    Ignore, watch or act, with the companies it touches, a time horizon and a one-line reason. Routine filings are set aside, and you can see why.

  3. 03

    It narrows to the stocks you own

    Paste your holdings or import your broker's CSV. My stocks shows only news about the companies you own. Your holdings stay in your browser.

  4. 04

    You're prepared before 9:15

    One checklist of the overnight moves that set the tone for Indian stocks: US close, Asia, the dollar and rupee, crude and India VIX.

Three verdicts

One word tells you what to read. No scores, no stars.

Each verdict carries its reason and a time horizon, so you can check it in seconds, and disagree when you know better.

ACT

Material to a holding

Results, guidance changes, regulatory orders or deals that change the picture for a company.

e.g. a pharma company receives a USFDA warning letter for its main plant

WATCH

Could become material

Early signals that need a second data point: a pledge change, a management exit, a draft circular.

e.g. promoter pledge rises sharply in a quarter

IGNORE

Noise for your portfolio

Routine compliance filings, recycled stories and market chatter with no new information.

e.g. Regulation 74(5) certificate, trading window closure

      Questions

      Before you start. Quick answers.

      Is PulsEdge investment advice?

      No. PulsEdge is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. Verdicts are automated triage of public news that tell you what to read, not what to buy or sell. Always check the source and make your own decision.

      Where does the news come from?

      Public sources: NSE corporate announcements, SEBI and RBI releases, and markets coverage from Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard and Moneycontrol. Every item links back to where it was published.

      How are verdicts made?

      Each headline is matched to Nifty 500 companies and classified as ignore, watch or act, with a horizon and a one-line reason. During the beta some verdicts come from rules and some from an AI model, and each verdict says which. The track record shows how they've done.

      How does the free trial work?

      Enter your email in the terminal and you get 5 NSE trading sessions, usually one calendar week. Weekends and market holidays don't count. No card is needed and nothing is charged when it ends.

      Do you see my holdings?

      No. Holdings you add, typed or imported from a CSV, are saved in your own browser and never uploaded to us.

      Which stocks are covered?

      The Nifty 500, refreshed weekly from NSE's official list. That covers most of the market's value and the stocks most portfolios hold.

      Can I cancel or get a refund?

      Yes. Cancel any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If a paid plan isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund. Details are in the refund policy.
